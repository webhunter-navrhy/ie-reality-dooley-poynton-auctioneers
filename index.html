Description

Dooley Poynton Auctioneers are delighted to present for sale two exclusive, architecturally designed homes in the heart of the charming village of Redcross, Co. Wicklow.

Finished to an exceptional standard and achieving an impressive A rated BER, these outstanding homes combine contemporary comfort, energy efficiency and timeless appeal.

Ideally positioned within Redcross Village, residents will enjoy immediate access to a host of local amenities, including schools, crèches, shops and community services. The M11 motorway is just five minutes away, providing excellent connectivity to Arklow, Wicklow Town and South Dublin. Set amidst the stunning natural landscape of County Wicklow, Redcross offers an enviable balance of rural tranquility and modern convenience, a welcoming community and an ideal setting for contemporary family living.
